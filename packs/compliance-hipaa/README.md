# `pack-compliance-hipaa`

Opt-in HIPAA-oriented contracts for minimum-necessary PHI handling,
purpose-bound access and tamper-evident incident evidence.

## Use it

Tag a project with `hipaa`, `phi`, `healthcare`, `health-data` or
`regulated-health` and install the pack through the normal `aqa` pack workflow.

## Evidence boundary

This pack is an engineering baseline, not a HIPAA attestation, legal opinion,
Business Associate Agreement, HITRUST certification or breach-notification
determination. It uses seeded non-production fixtures and does not prove
facility, workforce, vendor, physical, contractual or jurisdictional controls.
Bind it to approved identity, audit, retention and incident-response systems;
obtain independent compliance and legal review separately.
