# ADR-221: Ship an opt-in HIPAA-oriented safety pack

## Status

Accepted — 2026-09-18

## Decision

Ship `pack-compliance-hipaa` with contracts for minimum-necessary PHI,
purpose-bound access and tamper-evident incident evidence.

## Boundary

The pack is not a HIPAA attestation or legal determination. Administrative,
physical, contractual, vendor and jurisdiction-specific evidence remain
separate boundaries.
